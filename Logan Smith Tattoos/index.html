FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your appointment.

Simply fill out the contact form below with your tattoo idea, preferred placement, and approximate size. I'll get back to you within 24-48 hours to schedule a free consultation where we can discuss your vision in detail.

Bring any reference images, ideas, or inspiration you have. Even if you don't know exactly what you want, photos of styles you like help me understand your taste. We'll work together to create something perfect for you.

My hourly rate is $150. Small pieces (2-3 hours) typically range from $300-$450. Larger projects like sleeves are quoted per project. I'll always give you a clear estimate before we begin so there are no surprises.

Pain tolerance varies from person to person. Most clients describe it as an uncomfortable scratching sensation rather than sharp pain. I take regular breaks and work at a pace that keeps you comfortable throughout the session.

You'll receive detailed aftercare instructions after your session. Generally, keep the bandage on for 2-4 hours, wash gently with unscented soap, apply a thin layer of recommended ointment, and avoid sun exposure and swimming for 2 weeks.

I work by appointment only to ensure every client gets my full attention and custom designs. This allows me to prepare properly for each session and deliver the best possible results.
